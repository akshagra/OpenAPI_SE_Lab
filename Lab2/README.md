## What Was Done

- Turned the Lab 1 requirements into a product backlog of 13 user stories (for example *Upload OpenAPI Spec*, *Validate Every Response Against Schema*, *Sustain 1,000 RPS With Accurate Latency*, *Configure Access Control*). Each story has a priority (High, Medium or Low) and story points from 0 to 8.
- Planned two sprints: Sprint 1 with 3 stories and Sprint 2 with the remaining 10 (about 42 points).
- Simulated progress on the sprint boards and tracked Sprint 2 with a burndown chart.

## Reflection Highlights

- **Estimation:** The points didn't always match the real complexity. *Sustain 1,000 RPS* was left at 0 points even though it is one of the hardest items, while routine stories were estimated at 8.
- **Prioritization:** Ordering by priority made sense, but the sprint split was unbalanced and most of the backlog ended up in Sprint 2.
- **Sprint outcome:** Sprint 1 completed all 3 stories. At the time of the snapshot, Sprint 2 had 3 of 10 stories done, which shows it was overcommitted.
- **Burndown:** The chart starts at 42 points and the actual line is still near the start. That points to the same overcommitment as the board.

**Conclusion:** The main issue was estimation, not capacity. The next sprint should re-estimate the 0-point items and keep each sprint's total close to the velocity shown in Sprint 1.
